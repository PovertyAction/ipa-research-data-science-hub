# About Communicating and Using Data

Overview of IPA’s data communication resources, covering how to turn analysis into clear messages, design principles for visualization, chart selection, dashboards that drive decisions, and using evidence for impact.

Sound analysis does not speak for itself. These pages cover the communication side of data work: defining the message, choosing the right visual form, and designing outputs that lead to decisions rather than merely displaying numbers.
