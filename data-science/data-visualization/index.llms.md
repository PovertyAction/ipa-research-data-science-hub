# Data Visualization

This section covers both sides of data visualization: how to communicate and use data for decisions -why, for whom, and with what message-, and how to build the visualizations in Python. Start with the concepts, then move to the tools.

Strong analysis only creates value once someone understands it and makes a better decision because of it. These pages cover reading your audience, choosing a clear form, and designing honest visuals, along with the relationships and tools that let evidence inform real decisions, and then how to produce those visuals in code.

## Communicating and using data

The conceptual, tool-agnostic layer, useful whether or not you write code. Start here.

- **[Communicating with Data](../../data-science/data-visualization/communicating/communicating-with-data.llms.md)**. Why communication is part of the analysis, reading your audience, and the *who*, *what*, *how* framework.
- **[Data Visualization Principles](../../data-science/data-visualization/communicating/data-visualization-principles.llms.md)**. Decluttering, human perception, Tufte’s data-ink rule, and responsible color.
- **[Choosing the Right Chart](../../data-science/data-visualization/communicating/choosing-the-right-chart.llms.md)**. Table versus graph, which chart fits which message, and when to use a map.
- **[Turning Data into Impact](../../data-science/data-visualization/communicating/turning-data-into-impact.llms.md)**. Why evidence goes unused, and how audience mapping, tailoring, and timing close the gap.
- **[Dashboards for Decisions](../../data-science/data-visualization/communicating/monitoring-dashboards.llms.md)**. Design monitoring dashboards that help decision-makers choose what to do next.

## Creating visualizations with Python

The hands-on layer, a workshop for producing publication-quality figures with `seaborn.objects` and the grammar of graphics. Best read after the concepts above.

- **[Introduction to Seaborn](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/intro-seaborn-objects.llms.md)**. Why visualization matters in research, and your first plot with the `seaborn.objects` interface.
- **[The Grammar of Graphics](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/grammar-of-graphics.llms.md)**. Map data variables to visual properties like color, size, and shape.
- **[Marks and Geometric Objects](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/marks-geometric-objects.llms.md)**. Dots, lines, bars, areas, and bands, and how to combine them in layers.
- **[Labels, Scales, and Customization](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/labels-scales-customization.llms.md)**. Titles, legends, axis limits, palettes, and accessibility.
- **[Faceting and Layering](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/faceting-layering.llms.md)**. Small multiples and combining plot types into rich displays.
- **[Statistical Transformations](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/statistical-transformations.llms.md)**. Aggregations, confidence intervals, regression lines, and histograms.
- **[Themes and Final Polish](../../data-science/data-visualization/python/themes-final-polish.llms.md)**. Professional themes and saving publication-quality figures.
