# About Research Transparency

Overview of IPA’s research transparency resources, covering open science principles, preparation of research materials for public sharing, and publishing replication packages to Dataverse.

Research transparency makes the full research process visible and verifiable: how a study was designed, how data were collected and analyzed, and how findings can be reproduced. Open science practices strengthen the credibility of research and let others build on it.
