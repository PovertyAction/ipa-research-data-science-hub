# About Survey and Respondent Tracking

Overview of IPA’s tracking resources for fieldwork, covering survey progress tracking, respondent tracking, longitudinal strategies for panel studies, replacement procedures, and mop-up operations.

Tracking systems monitor two things at once: how fieldwork is progressing overall, and what happened to each individual respondent. Together they prevent sample loss, minimize attrition, and protect the representativeness of your data—especially in panel studies that return to the same respondents over multiple rounds.
