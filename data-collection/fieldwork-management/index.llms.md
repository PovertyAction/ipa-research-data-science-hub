# About Fieldwork Management

Overview of IPA’s fieldwork management resources, covering field staff training, community engagement, surveyor performance management, paper survey data entry, and survey and respondent tracking.

Successful data collection depends on well-managed fieldwork: trained field teams, good relationships with the communities where research happens, clear performance expectations, and systems that track every survey and respondent. The guides in this section cover the operational side of running field data collection.

## Getting Started

**Planning fieldwork?** Start with [Field Staff Training](../../data-collection/fieldwork-management/field-staff-training.llms.md) and [Community Entry](../../data-collection/fieldwork-management/community-entry-local-leaders.llms.md) before teams deploy, then set up [tracking systems](../../data-collection/fieldwork-management/tracking/index.llms.md) before the first interview.
